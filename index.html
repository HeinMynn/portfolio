Switchboard / Product preview01

Local account switching for Chrome & Firefox.

01 / The problem

One site, multiple identities.

Repeated sign-ins interrupt the workflow. Saved accounts need to be easy to find and deliberate to switch.

02 / The approach

Use the browser's strengths.

Chrome saves and restores named cookie snapshots. Firefox uses native containers to keep accounts open side by side.

03 / The tradeoff

Keep the boundaries clear.

Chrome has one active account per site. Cookies alone cannot restore every site's session; a recovery journal protects interrupted switches.
